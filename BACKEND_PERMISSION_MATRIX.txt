MALENGE FARMERS CRM - BACKEND PERMISSION MATRIX
Phase 6 Accountability Core - 10 September 2026

SYSTEM ADMIN
- System-wide technical administration only.
- Users/access, executive appointments/history, cooperative structure, security,
  audit logs, system health, exports/settings and recovery operations.
- Does NOT perform cooperative Secretary, Treasurer or Chairperson governance duties.
- Does not become a cooperative executive merely because Admin can inspect system audit logs.

ALL COOPERATIVE EXECUTIVES
- May view the Accountability Register and Meetings & Evidence for their OWN cooperative.
- May view official evidence, resolutions, responsible offices, deadlines, progress and verification history.
- May not mutate another cooperative's accountability records.

SECONDARY / PRIMARY CHAIRPERSON
- Confirms and locks official meeting evidence for own cooperative.
- Certifies structured resolutions captured from confirmed meeting evidence.
- Certification creates the accountability task for the named responsible executive.
- May independently verify completed accountability work when not the responsible person.
- Confirms/rejects Treasurer finance belonging to own cooperative only.
- Cannot self-verify an accountability task assigned to the Chairperson.

SECONDARY / PRIMARY VICE CHAIRPERSON
- Leadership/operational oversight.
- Carries out accountability tasks assigned to the Vice Chairperson office.
- Only the responsible executive may report progress/upload proof for that assigned task.
- May independently verify another executive's completed work when not self-verifying.
- No finance confirmation unless a future delegation policy explicitly grants it.

SECONDARY SECRETARY / VICE SECRETARY
- Registers Secondary meeting indexes and uploads handwritten/signed evidence.
- May edit meeting metadata only while the meeting is Draft.
- Captures actionable resolutions from confirmed meeting evidence.
- May edit resolutions only while they are Draft.
- Secretary may independently verify another executive's completed accountability work.
- Vice Secretary does not independently close accountability work in this Phase 6 core.
- Existing network membership oversight rules remain unchanged.

PRIMARY SECRETARY / VICE SECRETARY
- Registers and maintains members/farmer-person records for assigned Primary only.
- Registers meeting indexes and uploads handwritten/signed evidence.
- May edit meeting metadata only while the meeting is Draft.
- Captures actionable resolutions from confirmed meeting evidence.
- May edit resolutions only while they are Draft.
- Secretary may independently verify another executive's completed accountability work.
- Vice Secretary assists but does not independently close accountability work in this Phase 6 core.
- Cannot record actual membership-fee money.

SECONDARY / PRIMARY TREASURER
- Existing finance recording rules remain in force for the Treasurer's own cooperative.
- Can view governance evidence and accountability records for own cooperative.
- Carries out accountability tasks assigned to the Treasurer office and uploads proof.
- Cannot verify their own accountability work.
- Cannot confirm their own financial entries.

ACCOUNTABILITY PROTECTION RULES
- Official handwritten/printed/signed meeting pages are uploaded; full minutes do not need to be retyped.
- Confirmed meeting evidence has no silent replace/delete route.
- Uploaded files are stored outside /static, file-signature checked and SHA-256 fingerprinted.
- A meeting recorded as Quorum Not Met cannot produce a certified action resolution.
- Certified resolutions cannot be edited through the draft resolution editor.
- Resolution-linked tasks cannot be edited/deleted through the legacy Operations Tasks module.
- The responsible executive is the only executive allowed to report progress/upload proof for their task.
- Completed work becomes Awaiting Verification instead of immediately closing.
- Self-verification is blocked.
- Verification requires at least one evidence file and a verification note.
- Returned work reopens In Progress and preserves the verifier's reason in permanent progress history.
- Verified work closes the task and source resolution.
- State changes create Audit Log events and remain cooperative-scoped.
